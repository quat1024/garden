# Misinformation about Trans Lifeline's number

I have seen people sharing **877-330-6366** as a "Canadian LGBTQ hotline" which they have "opened/donated to Americans". This is **false**.

* This number belongs to a **Trans Lifeline**, a nonprofit. It does not belong to "Canada".
* It is not an "LGBTQ hotline", it is a *trans hotline*.
* Trans Hotline has a perfectly good US number: **(877)-565-8860**.
* Trans Hotline is *not* in danger of shutting down.
	* It is not part of the 988 system. They have never accepted federal hotline funding.
	* Instead, Trans Hotline is funded *by us:* https://translifeline.org/donate/

Mislabeling their number as a general "LGBTQ hotline" has caused problems for them and their callers.

> *This misinformation has unfortunately led to a sharp increase in calls from people outside of our community, which limits our capacity to support the trans people our line was built to serve.*

[Source.](https://translifeline.org/clarifying-misinformation-about-our-hotline-number/)

> *Our hotline is being incorrectly shared as "Canada's national suicide hotline open toll free for Americans". We are not a general crisis hotline, we exist exclusively to support the trans community.*

[Source.](https://www.facebook.com/TransLifeline/posts/-misinformation-alert-our-hotline-is-being-incorrectly-shared-as-canadas-nationa/1106887618150526/)

(Is this an attempt to trick well-meaning people into flooding a trans hotline with calls they can't accept? Well, maybe.)

## They're not going anywhere

["We will continue to provide peer-to-peer support for our community well beyond this Pride. Our hotline is funded by grassroots support, not the government, so we'll be staying open as long as we're needed."](https://bsky.app/profile/translifeline.org/post/3lsew24tvus27).

## Regarding police

Here is another piece of misinformation I found in a Facebook comment:

> *"I feel the Canadian line is at least not likely to call the police."*

Trans Hotline will not call the police on you unless you ask. They have been sharply critical of 988's practice of nonconsensual police interventions, and have started a [\#SafeHotlines movement](https://translifeline.org/safe-hotlines/) against this practice.

See [the Trans Hotline FAQ](https://translifeline.org/hotline/#faq).

> **Will you connect or call the police/emergency services during my call?**
> 
> Trans Lifeline has a policy against nonconsensual active rescue. We will not call emergency services or law enforcement without your explicit request – even if you tell us you or someone else is in danger.
> 
> If you would like support requesting an ambulance or calling 911, we can assist you after informing you about that process and what may be triggered.
