# Always a student

It's a lot easier for people to *say* "I'm always a student" than to always *be* a student. Maybe that's how I feel, anyway.

What does it *mean* to always be a student,  besides "wanting to learn a lot", of course.

* Deferrent. Do not relive the asshole freshman years.
* Curious. Sometimes the outsider perspective actually *is* helpful.
* Open minded. There's lots of different avenues to approach a problem from.
* Closed minded. Focus on *your* lane, do the work, and you will get things done.

Wanting to learn a lot isn't enough. You have to *actually* learn a lot. There are a lot of activities that feel like learning but are not learning.
