# Mod Sources and Issues

I am migrating off of Github but I'm still committed to working in the open.

## "Season 2" mods

All mods with a year in their version number are part of [Season 2](/mods/season2), and are available on:

* Codeberg: https://codeberg.org/quat/mods
* Tangled: https://tangled.org/@highlysuspect.agency/mods
  * You can sign in to Tangled through the AT protocol (e.g. Bluesky) to report issues.
  
I will accept bug reports through whichever platform you find best. If you use Tangled, ping me on bluesky afterwards (I don't think they have notifications).
